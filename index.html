Tracker User Guide & Features

Per-Child Custom Action Tiles

Customize which quick action tiles (Nursing, Sleep, Bottle, Diaper) appear on the dashboard for each child:

  1. Tap the Settings (Gear) icon in the top bar.
  2. Find your child's profile card.
  3. Toggle individual action tiles on or off depending on what you need to track.

Filtering Logs

Narrow down your log history using the filter panel above the Logs Feed:

  • Type Filter: Filter entries by Nursing, Sleep, Bottle, or Diaper.
  • Date Filter: Choose All Time, Today, Last 7 Days, or select a custom date range.

CSV Export & Import Instructions

Back up or restore your tracking history using CSV files:

  • Exporting: Open Settings and tap Export CSV to save your tracking data locally.
  • Importing: Open Settings, select your formatted CSV file, and tap Import CSV.
